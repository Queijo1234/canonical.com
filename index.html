§ 01

About

Belo Horizonte → Paris

I am a 17-year-old undergraduate student in Physics at Université Paris-Saclay, originally from Belo Horizonte, Brazil. My path to research was unconventional.

After being admitted to both ITA and IME — two of Brazil's most selective engineering institutions — I chose to leave in my first year and move to France. That decision was not an escape; it was a commitment. I realized that what drives me is not engineering as a profession, but physics as a way of thinking.

I wanted to follow curiosity, not a career track — so I left.

My long-term goal is to pursue a PhD and contribute to fundamental research — the kind of science that asks questions before it knows whether the answers will be useful.
